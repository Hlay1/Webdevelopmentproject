Projects

Frankencycle

While in highschool, I was given an old quad, but only two tires held air. Naturally I cut and welded the frame to accomodate the loss of two wheels, and I ended up with a homemade fat tire dirtbike!

Pickup Truck

I have had this truck for several years now, and it hasn't always looked this nice. I bought it for $500 after it had been wrecked and left to die in a field. I was able to repair the frame and some other systems, and now it runs great! I have also done some minor modifications such as upgrading the wheels and tires, making a new lower facia with a bull bar and lights, and fun things like replacing the shift knobs.
